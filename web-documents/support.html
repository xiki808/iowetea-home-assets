IOweTea Home Support

For help with the IOweTea Home mobile app or your server, email home.support@iowetea.com. Include your phone platform, app version, and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, VPN private keys, or Wi-Fi passwords.

Getting started

Open the app and follow Setup IOweTea Home to connect it to your IOweTea Home server. If you do not have a server, select Try the demo on the setup screen to explore a sample home without an account.

Privacy and accounts

Read the privacy policy or the account-deletion instructions. You can also request account deletion by email at the address above.
